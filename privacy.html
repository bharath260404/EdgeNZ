Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Callroll.ai ("we", "us") provides attendance and parent follow-up software to schools and colleges ("institutions"). This policy explains what data we handle, why, and your choices.

1. Who controls the data

Each institution decides what student and parent data it puts into Callroll.ai and how it is used. For that data the institution is the data fiduciary (controller) and Callroll.ai processes it on the institution's behalf, under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable Indian law.

2. Data we process

  • Staff accounts: name, email or username, role, and the classes allotted to them.
  • Students: name, roll number, class and section or course, and attendance records.
  • Parents or guardians: name, phone and WhatsApp number, and preferred language.
  • Follow-ups: WhatsApp message delivery status, call recordings or transcripts, and the absence reason given.
  • Website visitors: details you send through our contact form or email.

3. How we use it

  • To let staff record attendance and view reports.
  • To notify parents of absences by WhatsApp and phone call, and record the reason.
  • To keep the service secure, fix problems and provide support.

We do not sell personal data, show advertising, or use student or parent data for any purpose other than providing the service to the institution.

4. Service providers

We use trusted providers only to run the service: Meta Platforms (WhatsApp Business Platform) for messages, telephony providers for calls, speech and AI providers for transcription and conversation, and cloud hosting. They may process data only on our instructions.

5. Children's data

Student data is provided by the institution, which is responsible for obtaining any consent required from parents or guardians. We do not knowingly collect data directly from children.

6. Security

Data is kept separate for each institution. Access is limited by role and checked by our servers on every request. Data is encrypted in transit.

7. Retention

We keep data while the institution uses Callroll.ai. When an institution stops using the service, we delete or return its data within 90 days unless the law requires us to keep it longer.

8. Your rights

Parents and students can ask their institution to access, correct or delete their data. You can also contact us and we will pass the request to the institution and help it respond.
