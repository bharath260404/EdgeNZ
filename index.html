      FAQ

      Questions schools and colleges ask us.

      What works today, and what is coming?

      Available today: attendance for schools and colleges, timetables and classroom QR panels, reports and the monthly register, Excel import, study materials, tests and quizzes with AI question writing, marks and results, notices, the college student app, Studysy for school students, and the AI assistant for staff. Coming soon: WhatsApp alerts and replies from parents, then AI voice calls, and parent-teacher meetings.

      Do students or parents need to install anything?

      No. Students open their app in the browser on any phone, and can add it to their home screen. When parent follow-ups launch, parents will get a normal WhatsApp message.

      Does it work for colleges as well as schools?

      Yes. Schools mark one daily register per class. Colleges mark course by course, hour by hour, with a timetable, heads of department and course-wise shortage tracking. Junior colleges, degree and engineering colleges all fit.

      How does the AI work, and is there a limit?

      The AI writes test questions, answers students' doubts and answers staff questions from your data. It has fair daily limits so it stays available for everyone, and common questions are answered without AI at all.

      Can a teacher see other classes?

      No. Faculty only see the classes and courses allotted to them, and heads of department see their own department. This is checked by the server on every request.

      How long does setup take?

      Usually a day. Upload your students from Excel, add your faculty and courses, and you're marking attendance.

      What does it cost?

      Start with a free pilot for one class. After that, pricing is per student per year based on your size. Contact us for a quote.
