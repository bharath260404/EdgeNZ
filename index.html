    FAQ

    Questions schools ask us.

    What works today, and what is coming?

    Available today: fast attendance, reports, shortage tracking, printable monthly registers, Excel import, teacher accounts and the AI assistant for staff. Coming soon: WhatsApp alerts and replies from parents, then AI voice calls.

    Will parents need to install an app?

    No. When follow-ups launch, parents get a normal WhatsApp message.

    Which languages will the AI speak?

    Telugu is our first language, with Hindi and English next, followed by Tamil, Kannada, Malayalam, Marathi, Bengali, Gujarati, Punjabi, Urdu, Odia and Assamese. Each parent will be contacted in the language set on their student record.

    What if a parent doesn't answer?

    When follow-ups launch, the case will be marked for attention so staff can call personally.

    Can a teacher see other classes?

    No. Teachers only see the classes allotted to them, and this is enforced by the server on every request.

    How long does setup take?

    Usually a day. Upload your students from Excel, add your teachers, and you're live.

    What does it cost?

    Start with a free pilot for one class. After that, pricing is per student per year based on your size — contact us for a quote.
