    FAQ

    Questions schools ask us.

    Do parents need to install an app?

    No. They get a normal WhatsApp message and a normal phone call.

    Which languages does the AI speak?

    English, Hindi, Telugu and Tamil, with more Indian languages being added. Each parent is contacted in the language set on their student record.

    What if the parent doesn't answer?

    The case is marked for attention in your follow-up tracker, so staff can retry with one tap or call personally.

    Can a teacher see other classes?

    No. Teachers only see the classes allotted to them, and this is enforced by the server on every request.

    How long does setup take?

    Usually a day. We import your students from a spreadsheet, you add your teachers, and you're live.

    What does it cost?

    Start with a free pilot for one class. After that, pricing is per student per year based on your size — contact us for a quote.
